Chord-line Directive
====================

A legacy Ultimate-Guitar-style block: a chord row sits directly above its
lyric row and chords anchor to lyric characters strictly by column. A
``[Chorus]`` bracket line is a section header, not a chord.

.. chord-line::

   {title: Legacy Song}

   [Verse 1]
   Am      C
   Hello world here
   G        F
   Line two follows

   [Chorus]
   C       G
   Sing it out loud
